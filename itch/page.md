There is an object.

You can touch it.

It is liquid, and pale, and it sits in a white room. It has not said what it is. It does not have to.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/itch/pictures/room.png)

Touch it and it answers. Some answers you will find in the first minute. Some take longer, and some only come if you are patient, or careless, or cruel. It keeps count.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/devlog/2026-10-29/burst.gif)

It can be cut, and it is afraid after. It can come apart, and come home. It can go to dust, and what grows from the dust grows only as far as it is looked after. It scars.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/devlog/2026-10-29/cells.gif)

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/devlog/2026-10-29/tree.gif)

It remembers how it was treated, from one visit to the next.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/devlog/2026-10-29/scar.gif)

It is also paying attention. The longer you stay, the more it learns the way you touch it. You will not be the only one who is curious, and it will not always be the only one in the room.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniwan/the-object/main/itch/pictures/night.png)

Its memory of you lives in your browser and nowhere else.

Mouse, touch or keys. Sound helps; press **m** to mute. Needs a browser with WebGL2. Best in fullscreen.

Some of it only happens at its own address, **[the-object.antoniwan.online](https://the-object.antoniwan.online/)**. There it can hear you, in English and in Spanish, and it knows when it is being greeted. On a phone, it feels the room move. This page cannot let it hear you. Its memory there is its own: it will meet you as a stranger.

Written in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly, drawn by one shader. No engine. Source: [github.com/antoniwan/the-object](https://github.com/antoniwan/the-object)
