# Changelog

Every version that has been live, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the numbers follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html), read for a piece you touch rather than an API:

- **major**: the arc changes (how it learns you, what it does at the end), or something a player kept between visits is lost.
- **minor**: a new verb, a new form, or anything new a player can do or see.
- **patch**: fixes, speed, words, metadata, hosting and documentation.

Before 1.0.0, the first release outside its own site, everything new was a minor version, the arc included.

Every merge to `main` is deployed, so every pull request is a release and carries its own entry. `CONTRIBUTING.md` says how.

- A cut runs through the whole room for a moment; the burst throws every color out in rays. (#74)
- At night a ring of split light stands around it. (#74)

### Fixed

## [1.19.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- The flower, pinched a little too slowly, flinches and opens again. (#73)

### Changed
- Pulled the other way, it stands a little taller, and settles. (#73)

### Fixed
- Two fingers pinching a bud no longer flatten all of it. (#73)

## [1.18.1] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- Coming back to the tab no longer leaves it drawn coarser; after a slow moment it sharpens again, and the light under it fades rather than switching off. (#72)
- Gathered, it grows back among its pieces instead of appearing all at once. (#72)
- The eye closes instead of vanishing, and moves with the cells as they turn to grain. (#72)
- Becoming a box or dust, the light under it fades away instead of going out. (#72)

## [1.18.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Let go of a little too late, a bud nearly rises, and sinks back. (#71)
- Two fingers pinched together on it leave a bud between them. (#71)

### Fixed
- A quick short tug leaves a bud instead of throwing it. (#71)
- A pinch on a trackpad squeezes it as far as fingers on a screen; in Safari it no longer zooms the page. (#71)
- A quick tap that slides a little still touches it. (#71)

## [1.17.2] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- At night a phone's own bars darken with the room. (#70)
- If you ask your device for less motion, the room shakes and flashes less and the words stop breathing. (#70)

### Fixed
- Leaving the tab no longer makes it click; its sound fades out, and fades back in when you return. (#70)
- A pinch while it loads no longer leaves the page zoomed. (#70)

## [1.17.1] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- It hears quieter sounds. A whisper at arm's length from a phone is enough now, and so is a soft voice. It learns how loud the room is on its own, so a fan or a street is only the room, and a voice over it is still someone.
- It listens for words in English and in Spanish. It starts in the browser's own language and keeps the other one near; when what it hears keeps making no sense, it listens in the other, and stays where it last understood. Spanish words with a pronoun on the end count, and it knows more of them in both languages than before. Of the browser's guesses at what was said, it takes the one it understands.

## [1.17.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- A small microphone waits in the bottom-left corner from the first moment of every visit. Tapped, the browser asks; allowed, it fades and the mark for hearing comes in its place. Stopped, it comes back. It is never shown where the page cannot hear, such as the copy on itch.io, or after a no. Where words must leave the page to be made, it asks for them as soon as it hears you, not only late in the arc.

## [1.16.7] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- It wakes again in Safari, on the iPhone and the Mac. Since 1.12.1 the page stopped on load there with "Array.from requires an array-like object", before anything appeared. Every change is now loaded in a few browsers, a first visit and a return, before it goes live.

## [1.16.6] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Its own count of visits beside the one it had, with no cookies, that leaves alone anyone who asks not to be followed. It also notes when someone leaves by a link. The copy on itch.io leaves it out. (#66)

## [1.16.5] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- The devlog is one post a day now, for every day something changed, with short moving pictures of what is new. A version no longer waits for a post of its own; the tests fail once a day has gone by without one. (#65)
- Those pictures are filmed the same way every time, from scripted hands on a clock that only moves when it is told to. (#65)

## [1.16.4] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- The script that sends the answer to its question is its own file now, so the copy on itch.io leaves it out by name. The page is otherwise the same. (#64)
- Every change is now checked for warnings and timed the same way, and builds reuse what they built before. (#64)

## [1.16.3] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- The code that makes it says each small thing once: shared pieces of arithmetic live in one place, what only one part uses is kept inside that part, and every value handed to the drawing goes the same way. The browser's own libraries are only built for the browser. Nothing it does, looks like or sounds like has changed. (#63)

## [1.16.2] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- The four largest parts of the code that makes it are each split into a folder of smaller files, one for each thing they do. Nothing it does, looks like or sounds like has changed, and every change from now on is checked against where it ended up before. (#62)

## [1.16.1] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- The code that makes it is sorted into folders by what each part does: what it becomes, what reaches it, what it learns, and the browser. Nothing it does, looks like or sounds like has changed. (#61)

## [1.16.0] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- The page lost its newest parts in a merge, and the object stayed on the other side; they are back. (#60)

### Changed
- Divided, it is no longer pink, and nothing sits in the middle of each part. Each one is pale, drawn over in lines where a slow wave over it is still, and the lines show what the cut did. Right after a cut they are cold, fine and trembling, and the surface shivers. Left alone, they calm, slow and thicken; then the ground deepens and the lines burn from inside, in time with a slow swell. Every new cut frightens it again from wherever it had got to. (#60)
- The one that looks is drawn the same way: all its lines run to the point it looks from. (#60)
- Going from parts to grain, and from one fineness of grain to the next, the drawing now blends instead of changing at once. (#60)

## [1.15.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Once the mound has settled, something takes root in it. Tended, it grows: a shoot, a trunk, branches one after another, pale leaves, and at last the flower's pink again, in blossom along them. It sways a little once it is tall. (#59)
- Left untended, it slowly draws back down to a shoot. Held, the mound takes it back in as the liquid rises out. Three new discoveries. (#59)

## [1.14.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- When it asks to hear or to see you and the moment goes by, the asking does not go with it: a faint mark waits in the corner for the rest of the visit, and taking it is the same as taking the word. Stopped, it waits there again. (#57)

### Changed
- Its word, when it asks, reaches further than it looks, so a thumb finds it; on a keyboard, Enter takes it. (#57)
- On a phone nothing under a finger is selected, flashed or called out, the page no longer pulls or bounces, and the corners keep clear of rounded screens and the home bar. (#57)
- Left in another tab, it falls silent, and its sound comes back with you. (#57)
- A hand closed on it shows as closed until it opens. (#57)
- A screen reader hears what it finds, thinks and asks, in plain letters, instead of the signs they pass through. (#57)

## [1.13.1] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- Since 1.11.0 the page could fall over as it opened, in browsers built on Chrome's engine that are not Chrome itself. It no longer asks anything about words until it has been let hear you, and then asks Chrome alone whether they can be made on the device. (#58)

## [1.13.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Hurt enough, over time, it scars where it is struck most, and the scar stays between visits. Hurt again, the scar opens, and small spiked pieces of it fall onto the floor around it, each slowly turning through every color. Some of what is carried back in is kept, and fills the wound; the rest is thrown back out. Filled, it closes over, and the scar is fainter. It never goes. Four discoveries come with it. (#56)

## [1.12.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Pinched once it has flowered, it does not close: it comes apart into a cloud of grain that settles over the floor in drifts. (#55)
- A finger drawn through the dust sweeps it along, and drifts pushed together become one. Gathered nearly all in one place, it heaps itself into a mound that settles, stands on the ground and breathes there. Left alone long enough, the dust drifts together on its own. (#55)
- Tapped, the mound answers low; held, light comes up through it and it rises back out of itself, floating again, and leaves nothing behind. Four new discoveries. (#55)

## [1.11.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Once it hears you, some words do what a hand would, as many times as they are said, in English or Spanish.
- A greeting, in almost any language, is answered in its own signs, the same signs for the same greeting; but only if it remembers being treated gently. Hurt lately, it draws back. Not sure of you yet, it only listens. Being greeted is remembered too.
- Three new things to find.

### Changed
- Words are made on the device where the browser can, so nothing leaves the page. Where the browser cannot, it asks for that separately, and says under its word where the voice would go.

## [1.10.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- It remembers what was done to it, between visits: being held, sung to, hummed to, answered and returned to, and being hurt or broken. Hurt fades sooner than care. Once it has begun to regard you, the first time a hand comes near on a visit, it leans toward a hand that was kind to it and pulls away from one that was not. Two discoveries come with it. (#53)

## [1.9.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- After it bursts, the twelve need not stay twelve, or one. Set close together they keep together, in as many groups as there are places to leave them. A group comes along when one of it is carried, unless it is pulled away faster than the rest can follow; a quick cut across the ground between them parts a group, each side stepping back. Touch one of a group and the rest feel it after, one by one. Each group breathes in its own time and slowly turns its faces inward. (#52)
- Five more things to find. (#52)

### Changed
- Carried to the middle together, a group goes home together. Only all twelve crowded into one still run as a river. (#52)

## [1.8.5] - 2026-10-29

## [1.8.3] - 2026-10-28

### Changed
- Each frame costs about two fifths less to draw: every ray starts where something could be and stops past the last of it, leaps the empty space between, and strides further where it safely can. Shapes, light and color are as they were. (#49)
- The room through the mirrors costs about a third less a frame, and the light and color laid over everything a quarter less, worked out once and shared instead of again for each surface. (#49)
- It takes no longer to get ready, though it draws far less each frame. (#49)

## [1.8.2] - 2026-10-28

### Changed
- Sooner on the screen: the words on the first screen carry only the letters it uses, 50 KB of type down to 33 KB, drawn exactly as before.
- A second visit downloads almost nothing: the object and its type are named by what is in them and kept for a year, so only a change is fetched again.
- The question it asks after contact checks its old answers only once it has appeared, never while it is still arriving.
- The icons and the picture for shared links are 31 KB lighter, pixel for pixel the same.

## [1.8.1] - 2026-10-28

### Changed
- Less to download: the drawing instructions travel without their notes, 151 KB to 145 KB gzipped. (#47)
- Lighter frames: the page is touched only when something on it changes, sounds that hold steady are no longer re-sent, and the picture from the camera reuses its room; about a sixth fewer calls between the two sides each frame, half as many on each pointer move. (#47)
- The motion itself costs about a third less a frame, and listening about a third less, with every movement exactly as it was. (#47)

## [1.8.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Its universe, stirred on the same way long after it holds one, gets out: the floor goes, the white room comes apart along the seams of a sky cut by mirrors, and it is everywhere, in every direction, each copy turning its own galaxy. (#46)
- In there, a poke gives the room another mirror; a hand held still runs its time backward until it is let go; the other way round folds the room back up around one of it. (#46)
- Four discoveries for it, and five thoughts for a universe that has not been let out. (#46)

## [1.7.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Every shock throws a ring of color out from it, a kick of light and a wash in the color of what happened: pink for a cut, cold for a fall, gold for the lid, red for the river's beat, every color for the burst. (#45)
- Upset, the edges of the room bruise; wounded, they beat, and something hot shows through it from inside. (#45)
- Light passes through it: the room bent through its body in split color, and gathered on the floor in its shadow with a ring of color around it. (#45)

### Changed
- Its skin carries a moving film of whole colors, faint across its face and strong at the edges, brighter while it looks at you; at night its rim takes the film's colors and the stars come back off it. (#45)
- The room's lights are sharper in its reflections, with a cold strip and a warm one low on either side. (#45)

## [1.6.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Late, after it has reached back once (or after fifty discoveries), something else comes down into the room: small, hard, dark and many-sided, with colors splitting at its edges. It never touches the floor. (#44)
- Tap it and it rings, and the first shivers. Carry it close and the first reaches for it. Throw it at the first and they clash. (#44)
- Press the two together and the first takes it in, a dark shape seen through it, and cools; squeeze and it is given back, each keeping some of the other's color. (#44)
- Hurt the first again and again while it is there and it comes between you. Leave them both alone and they circle. Eight new discoveries and five new thoughts. (#44)

## [1.5.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- A phone in the hand is the room: tipped, the room tips with it, as far as it is tipped; level is however it was held. (#43)
- Shaken, it sloshes against the far wall from each jolt and is upset; keep on and it comes loose, drops flung the way it was thrown, until the room goes dark. (#43)
- Where the browser must ask before the page can feel the phone, it asks once a visit, from early in the arc: `hold the room.` A no is answered and never asked again. On a desk nothing changes. (#43)
- Three thoughts for a room that has never moved. (#43)

## [1.4.1] - 2026-10-28

### Changed
- On a first visit its thoughts come sooner and closer together; after that it waits as long as before. (#42)

## [1.4.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Late in the arc it asks to hear you (`speak.`) and later to see you (`show yourself.`), once a visit; only a tap on that word lets the browser ask. A no is answered in its voice and never asked again. (#41)
- Heard: a voice makes it tremble where the pitch lands, a note held and let go is hummed back, a whisper stills it, a breath blows a drop off it, a shout upsets it, a sudden sound makes it flinch. (#41)
- Seen: it turns toward where you move, flinches from fast movement, the room goes dark while the lens is covered, and the eye follows you. (#41)
- A quiet mark in the corner while it hears or sees; tapping it, pressing Escape or leaving the tab stops both. Sound and picture become a few numbers in the browser and are dropped; nothing is recorded or sent. (#41)

## [1.3.1] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Thoughts for the keyboard's verbs: letters that have not landed, a note it would sing back, a breath it would match, a room that could tip, and its word not yet said back. (#40)

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- When nothing has been found for a while and no hand is on it, a line of what it is thinking sometimes surfaces under it: about where it is, what it has not yet been, and later about you. Never twice in a visit, never how. (#39)

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-28

- Five discoveries for these, the last for whoever answers its word. (#38)

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Three ways to change it for good: cut it and it divides, throw it down hard and the floor takes it, wound it enough and it bursts into twelve. (#1, #3)

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-28

- Sound, all synthesized.
- Discoveries are kept between visits.
