# color

*version 1.7.0 · 28 october 2026*

The room was white and quiet. It still is, until something happens in it.

Now each shock leaves color behind: a ring thrown outward, a wash across the room, a different color for each kind of harm or change. Upset, the edges of the room bruise. Hurt, they beat.

Light goes through it now. The room seen through its body is bent and split, and under it the light gathers on the floor in a bright ring.

Its skin carries a moving film of color, faint in the middle and strong at the edges, stronger while it is looking at you.
