# it scars

*version 1.13.0 · 29 october 2026*

It scars now. Hurt it enough, over enough visits, and a thin seam stays where it was struck most. Time does not take it away.

Hurt it again, and the seam opens. Small spiked pieces of it fall out and lie on the floor around it, each slowly turning through every color.

It takes some of them back. The others it throws out, hard. What it keeps fills the wound, and when the wound is full it closes over.

The seam is thinner after. It is still there.
