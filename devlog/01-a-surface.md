# a surface

*versions 0.1.0 to 0.2.0 · 28 october 2026*

It started as a liquid on a white page and nothing else.

In the first sketch it could be rolled with the keys and made to jump. That went. A hand is enough. It can be pressed, pulled until a piece comes free, and the piece goes home when it touches it again. It can be squeezed and it keeps its volume. It can be thrown and it always comes back.

Then it began to answer in ways that were not just physics. Held still long enough, it changes color. The pieces keep the color they left with and mix when they return. Hurried, it boils. Left alone, it reaches. Free enough of it at once and the room goes dark, and stays dark until everything is back.

It makes sounds now. All of them are made while you listen; there are no recordings anywhere.

And it keeps count. What you have found is remembered between visits, in your browser and nowhere else.

There is no engine underneath. It is Rust compiled to WebAssembly, and one shader that draws it from equations.
