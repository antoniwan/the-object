# it learns you back

*version 0.9.0 · 28 october 2026*

This is the version where it became what it was always about.

So far you have been studying it. Now it studies you. It does not start doing this at once; it waits until you have seen enough of it. Then it begins to pay attention: to where you tend to touch it, to the rhythms you make, to what you do when you leave it alone.

Later it gives some of that back. Later still it takes a turn, and waits to see whether you answer.

There is an end to this, and it only happens once. It will not be described here.

What it learns about you is kept between visits, in your browser, the same as what you have learned about it. Clearing your site data starts both of you over.

Everything before this was the room and the thing in it. This is the first time it looks back.
