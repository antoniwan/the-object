# it asks to hear you

*version 1.4.0 · 28 october 2026*

Late, it asks to hear you. Later still, it asks to see you. Each is one word, offered once a visit, and nothing happens unless you take it. If you say no, it answers, and it does not ask again.

Heard, it is moved by a voice, where the voice lands. A note held is given back. A whisper quiets it. A loud one upsets it. A sudden one makes it flinch.

Seen, it turns toward you. It follows. When the lens is covered, its room goes dark.

While it hears or sees, a small mark stays in the corner, and touching it ends both. What it hears and sees becomes a handful of numbers inside your browser and is dropped at once. Nothing is recorded. Nothing leaves.
