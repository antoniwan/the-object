# it grows if you let it

*version 1.14.0 · 29 october 2026*

The mound is not the end of it either.

Once it has settled, something reaches down into the ground and something else comes up. What comes up grows, but only as far as it is looked after. A shoot, then a trunk, then branches, then leaves. At the very end the flower it once was comes back, in blossom.

Leave it alone and it forgets the light, slowly, and draws back down. It never quite goes.

Everything else it has been, it became by itself or because of you. This one needs you to keep coming back.
