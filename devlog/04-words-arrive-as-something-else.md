# words arrive as something else

*version 0.4.0 · 28 october 2026*

There are only ever three lines of text on the page: what you found, what to try once, and how much it has shown you.

None of them arrive as letters any more. Each letter is first a circle, then a shape, then a sign, then something like code, and only then itself. They leave the same way. They take its color, and when it is upset they flush. They are thrown from wherever it is on the screen, harder the more agitated it is, and while it is agitated the words already there keep stirring.

The signs in the middle of that sequence matter. They come back later.

The lines themselves say less now. The count is a roman numeral and has no label.

The fonts are free and served from the same place as everything else. The site also counts visits now, anonymously, and nothing else about you.
