# it touched the ground

*version 1.12.0 · 29 october 2026*

The flower it can become is no longer the last thing it can be.

It can come apart. Not torn, not burst: let go of, all at once, until there is only a cloud, and then only dust lying on the white floor in drifts.

The dust can be moved. It goes where it is pushed, and it keeps to itself. Brought together, it heaps itself up into a mound that stands on the ground. Everything else it has been floats. This does not. It settles, and after a while it breathes.

It can come back up out of it. It leaves nothing behind.
