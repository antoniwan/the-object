# out of the room

*version 1.8.0 · 28 october 2026*

For a long time it has held a universe inside it. Now that universe can get out.

When it does, the floor goes and the white room comes apart along its seams. What is left is a sky cut by mirrors, and it is in every direction at once, every copy turning its own galaxy.

In there, time does not only go one way. And there is a way back to the room, folded up around one of it.
