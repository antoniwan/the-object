# a river, and the rules written down

*versions 1.1.0 to 1.1.2 · 28 october 2026*

The twelve can be crowded together, and if they are, they stop being twelve. They run as a river, dark red. Things stand up out of it. If enough of them stand at once, it beats. What comes back out of it afterwards is not the color it was, and keeps the rhythm for a while.

Behind the scenes, the way it looks, moves, sounds and speaks is now written down in one place, so that everything added later belongs to the same thing. The colors are in it, the timings, the sounds, and the rules for its voice. One of those rules changed today: late in the arc it may ask for something, in a word or two. It still never explains.

And it has version numbers now. This post is 1.1.2. Every change from the first afternoon has been counted back and given one, there is a changelog in the repository, and every version from here on carries its own entry. The next devlog will come with the next thing it learns to do.
