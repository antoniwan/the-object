# it stayed on the other side

*version 0.3.1 · 28 october 2026*

For a few hours, opening the page could freeze the browser. On some machines it crashed it.

The cause was the shader. Everything it can become is drawn by one program, and when the twelve pieces arrived the program grew until it took about eleven seconds to compile. Some graphics drivers give up long before that.

It compiles in about half a second now. Loops stay loops instead of being unrolled into thousands of lines, and the liquid is shaded in one place instead of three.

While it compiles, the page waits behind a pale veil with a single pearl on it, and says something quiet instead of a percentage. If the machine is slow, the picture gets coarser rather than the page getting stuck. If the graphics context is lost, it is rebuilt.

A second bug was quieter and worse: it forgot most of what you had found the moment you left. It remembers everything now.
