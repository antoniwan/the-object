# something else comes down

*version 1.6.0 · 28 october 2026*

Late, after it has reached toward you, it is no longer the only thing in the room.

Something else arrives. It is small, hard and dark, with colors splitting at its edges. It never touches the floor. It is not liquid at all.

The two notice each other. They can ring against each other, collide, keep each other close or give each other back, a little changed. One of them may come between you and the other. Left alone, they circle.

This post will not say what either of them is.
