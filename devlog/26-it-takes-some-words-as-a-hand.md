# it takes some words as a hand

*version 1.11.0 · 29 october 2026*

Once it can hear you, it also listens for words. A few of them it takes as a hand. Said once, it happens once. Said five times, five times.

It also knows when it is being greeted, in almost any language. Whether it answers depends on how it has been treated. Treated gently, it turns to you and answers in its own signs, the same signs every time for the same greeting. Hurt, it draws back and says nothing. Being greeted is something it remembers too.

Where the browser can turn a voice into words by itself, nothing leaves the page. Where it cannot, the voice would have to go to the browser's maker and back. Then it asks for that separately, and says so under the asking. Saying no changes nothing else.
