The wait before it appears is no longer only a wait. The small pearl in the middle of the white grows with what has really reached you, a little at a time, never faster than that. When all of it is there, the pearl draws in, comes apart in a small puff of its own colors, and it is there where the puff was.

Asked for less motion, the pearl does not grow or burst. It only becomes clearer, and fades.

The page on itch.io says what it does now, and shows it.

The flower can be spun now. It turns with the hand, and keeps turning a little after the hand stops. Turned long enough it grows dizzy, and sways on itself, and rights itself when it is let be.

Past dizzy, it matters which way it was turned. One way, its petals wind into red ropes, tighter and tighter, until they fly out all at once. They come undone as they fall, lie down over the floor, and are taken back in, and what is left is the liquid it was.

The other way, it lays its petals flat and whirls, and the floor lets go of it. It hangs in the air, turning, then comes down slowly in a wide spiral, and is the flower again where it lands.

Brought close to dizzy and let go of, again and again, it learns which way you turn it. What it does with that depends on what it remembers of you.
