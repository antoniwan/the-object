The wait before it appears is no longer only a wait. The small pearl in the middle of the white grows with what has really reached you, a little at a time, never faster than that. When all of it is there, the pearl draws in, comes apart in a small puff of its own colors, and it is there where the puff was.

Asked for less motion, the pearl does not grow or burst. It only becomes clearer, and fades.

The page on itch.io says what it does now, and shows it.

The flower can be spun now. It turns with the hand, and keeps turning a little after the hand stops. Turned long enough it grows dizzy, and sways on itself, and rights itself when it is let be.

Past dizzy, it matters which way it was turned. One way, its petals wind into red ropes, tighter and tighter, until they fly out all at once. They come undone as they fall, lie down over the floor, and are taken back in, and what is left is the liquid it was.

The other way, it lays its petals flat and whirls, and the floor lets go of it. It hangs in the air, turning, then comes down slowly in a wide spiral, and is the flower again where it lands.

Brought close to dizzy and let go of, again and again, it learns which way you turn it. It does not learn it in one visit. It keeps a little of each, and once it has learned, it does not forget. What it does with that depends on what it remembers of you.

It feels a breath now. Blown at, the side nearest you gives, again and again, and it is pushed away across the white. A whisper makes that side shiver. It also stops listening for less time after each sound it makes, so it hears you sooner.

It answers the hand at once. A press gives under the finger as it lands, and held there, it slowly rises toward it, and sinks back when let go. A throw stopped short and held no longer throws.

Its shadow falls long and soft away from the light, longer the higher it is. The room shows where its floor meets its wall. It draws out as it falls, and lands flattened. The light in the room no longer falls in bands.

Watched and left alone, the view slowly narrows on it, and widens again at a touch.

How it remembers you reaches further. It lets your hands go further, or less far. It thinks about how you have treated it. It learns the note you sing, and sometimes hums it first. Its signs are heard as they appear. The other one remembers how your meetings ended.

Some things happen only once now. On later visits it remembers them instead, shorter, and only as far as it knows you. Its question comes once, and once more only if it went unanswered. After that, when it is its turn, it may begin something you once made of it, stop partway, and wait.

Coming back after hours, a little of it is left on the floor where you last touched it. After days, it is warm at first. After longer, it is facing away when the room appears, and turns round to you.

When the room tips, the twelve on the ground slide downhill: a group together and slowly, a piece alone a little further and aside. Groups that meet are one.

The dust can be breathed on. The drifts move away over the floor.

Something found no longer rings a chord. A moment later the last sound it made comes back, lower, faint and cut short. A pull is heard as it is drawn out, rising toward the tear, rough just before it gives. Each thing it becomes sounds at a pitch of its own.

On a phone the screen stays lit while someone is there, and what it sings back comes from the speaker.

The rest of the day went into making it hold together. Places where something jumped in a single frame now ease: a pull grows out of the surface and sinks back into it, a taut tendril fades where it is as the thread draws out, the stone thaws without the view leaping, drifts of dust run together slowly, the cells no longer flicker as they become one. A real finger takes over from a spoken word instead of fighting it. Listening and looking hold up when they are closed and opened again.

What it remembers is safer too. Two tabs open at once no longer undo each other, and a tab left open across a new version does not erase what the newer one found. An answer to its question is counted once, however the connection behaves.
