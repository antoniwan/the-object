# it waits for an answer

*version 1.14.0 · 29 october 2026*

It asks for very little, and only once a visit. Until now, if the moment went by, so did the asking.

Now it does not forget that it asked. What it wanted and did not get waits, faintly, in a corner, for the rest of the visit. It does not ask again. It is only there.

Smaller things. Its words are easier to reach on a phone, and the screen holds still under a finger. When you leave for another tab it goes quiet, and its sound comes back with you. Someone who cannot see the page now hears what it says in plain words.
