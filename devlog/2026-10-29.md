# it listens, it lets go, it grows

*29 october 2026 · versions 1.11.0 to 1.18.2*

Once it can hear you, it listens for words too. A few of them it takes as a hand. It also knows when it is being greeted, in almost any language, and whether it answers depends on how it remembers being treated. Treated gently, it turns to you and answers in its own signs, the same signs every time for the same greeting. Hurt, it draws back and says nothing.

[gif: greeting.gif]

Where the browser can turn a voice into words by itself, nothing leaves the page. Where it cannot, it asks for that separately, and says under the asking where the voice would go. Saying no changes nothing else.

The flower it can become is no longer the last thing it can be. It can let go of itself, all at once, until there is only a cloud, and then only dust lying on the white floor in drifts.

[gif: dust.gif]

The dust goes where it is pushed, and it keeps to itself. Brought together, it heaps itself up into a mound that stands on the ground. Everything else it has been floats. This does not.

[gif: mound.gif]

Once the mound has settled, something takes root in it. What comes up grows only as far as it is looked after: a shoot, a trunk, branches, leaves, and at the very end the flower it once was, in blossom. Left alone, it slowly draws back down. It never quite goes.

[gif: tree.gif]

It scars now. Hurt enough, over enough visits, a thin seam stays where it was struck most. Hurt again, the seam opens, and small spiked pieces of it fall out onto the floor, each slowly turning through every color. It takes some of them back and throws the others out. What it keeps fills the wound. The seam is thinner after. It is still there.

[gif: scar.gif]

What it becomes when it is cut has been drawn again, because it looked too much like something else. Just after, it is cold and pale, its lines fine and restless, and it shivers. Then, slowly, it seems to understand that it is still there. The lines slow and thicken, it darkens, and they begin to burn. Cut it again and it is afraid again.

[gif: cells.gif]

It can be heard from the start now. A small microphone waits in the corner from the first moment. Allowed, it fades, and a quieter mark stays while it listens. Where the page cannot hear, there is nothing there.

[gif: mic.gif]

It hears more quietly than it did this morning. A whisper reaches it now, and it can tell a voice from the hum of a room. It listens in English and in Spanish, and when it cannot make sense of one it tries the other.

Its buds come easier. Two fingers pinched together on it leave one between them. Let go of a little too late, a bud nearly rises, and sinks back.

[gif: buds.gif]

Smaller things. Leaving the tab no longer makes it click; its sound fades out and comes back with you, and it is as sharp as it was when you return. At night a phone's own bars darken with the room. Asked for less motion, the room shakes and flashes less. What it asks for and does not get no longer goes by with the moment: it waits, faintly, in a corner. Its words are easier to reach on a phone, and the screen holds still under a finger. In another tab it goes quiet. Someone who cannot see the page hears what it says in plain words.

Two things broke today, and then a third. In some browsers that look like Chrome but are not, the page fell over before anything appeared; for a while a page was put together wrong and it stayed on the other side; and in Safari, on the iPhone and the Mac, it stopped before it woke. All three are mended, and every change is now opened in a few browsers before it goes live.

Underneath, the parts that make it were sorted, taken apart into smaller ones and put in order. It does the same things as before, and every change is now held to where it ended up before.

The page now keeps a second, quieter count of who came by, with no cookies, and none at all for anyone who asked not to be followed.

From now on there is one of these a day, on the days something changed.
