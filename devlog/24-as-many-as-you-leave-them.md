# as many as you leave them

*version 1.9.0 · 29 october 2026*

After it comes apart, the twelve pieces no longer have to choose between staying twelve and becoming one.

Left close together, some of them stay close. There can be two of them, or five, or several small groups in several places. A group moves when one of it is moved, though not always all of it. It can be parted again.

A group answers a touch together, one after another. Each one breathes in its own time, and over a while they turn toward each other.

Only all twelve, crowded into one, still do what they did before.
