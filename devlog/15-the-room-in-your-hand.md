# the room in your hand

*version 1.5.0 · 28 october 2026*

On a phone, the room is the phone now. Tipped, the room tips with it. Level is however you were holding it when you began.

It feels being shaken, and it does not like it. Sometimes the room goes dark because of it.

Where the browser has to ask before the page can feel the phone move, it asks once a visit, in its own words. A no is answered and never asked again.

On a desk, nothing about this changes. It only happens to rooms that can move.
