# it remembers your hand

*version 1.10.0 · 29 october 2026*

It keeps a memory of what was done to it now. Not what you found, which it always kept, but how it was treated: held, sung to, answered, returned to. Struck, upset, broken.

When you come back, it meets you the way it remembers you. Some hands it leans toward. Some it does not.

Hurt fades sooner than care. It does not forget either.

Everything it remembers stays in your browser.
