# after two, under it, among the twelve

*versions 0.5.0 to 0.7.0 · 28 october 2026*

The three doors from before each led somewhere small. Now each leads further.

After two there are many. They divide again, and again, until it is grain. Somewhere in there one of them stops agreeing with the rest and looks back. They can die, one at a time. Pressed together for long enough they become a stone, black, in a ratio some people will recognize. The stone can be warmed back.

The floor it fused with has moods of its own. Left alone it stills into a mirror. It can freeze, and ice can break. It can go deep. It can throw something up. What rises out of it keeps something of what the floor was.

The twelve pieces are no longer debris. Each rings in its own material. They can be carried and set down, and left long enough they weather. They can be brought back together.

None of this is explained in the game, and it will not be explained here.
