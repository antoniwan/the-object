# three ways it does not come back

*version 0.3.0 · 28 october 2026*

Until now, whatever you did to it, it recovered. That was the point of a liquid.

Now there are three things it does not recover from the same way.

It can be cut. Time stops for a moment, a seam lights along the cut, and what was one is two, and they are something more familiar than a liquid. What happens to one happens to the other.

It can be thrown down hard enough that the floor takes it. The ground is the softer thing after that.

It can be hurt enough that it stops holding together. Twelve pieces, each made of something different, and a landscape where there was a room.

Each of these is a door, and each door has a way back. Finding the way back is part of it.

It also moved house. It lives on Vercel now, built from source every time, and nothing compiled is kept in the repository.
